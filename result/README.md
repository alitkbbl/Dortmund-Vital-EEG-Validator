# Validation Case Study: Participant `sub-028`

This directory provides an end-to-end inspection sample generated by `dortmund-vital-eeg-validator` for participant **`sub-028`** of the **Dortmund Vital Study** ([OpenNeuro ds005385](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005385), v1.0.3).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

- **Overall Subject Status:** `PARTIALLY_USABLE`
- **Total Sessions / Runs Analyzed:** 8 raw EDF recordings (2 sessions × 2 tasks × 2 acquisitions)
- **Cumulative Raw Data Size:** ~392 MB (evaluated and dropped automatically post-analysis)
- **Key Findings:**
  - **Eyes-Closed Runs (`task-EyesClosed`):** **PASS / USABLE**. Strong, physiologically intact occipital alpha rhythm ($10.2\text{ Hz}$ at $O1, O2$), stable central midline electrodes ($Cz, Pz, Fz$), clean amplitude dynamics, and zero critical sensor dropouts.
  - **Eyes-Open Runs (`task-EyesOpen`):** **USABLE_NEEDS_INTERPOLATION** / **FLAGGED**. Multiple detached or high-impedance frontal electrodes detected (`F3, F4, F5, F6, AF7, AF8`) due to spatial decorrelation and excessive high-frequency noise ceilings.
  - **Scientific Implication:** Researchers studying resting-state alpha dynamics can confidently utilize the Eyes-Closed recordings; task-based or Eyes-Open analyses must interpolate the identified frontal cluster before feature extraction.

## 4. How to Inspect

1. **Quick Overview:** Open `status.txt` or examine `tables/run_level_summary.csv`.
2. **Spectral Verification:** View `figures/FigB_PSD_Spectra.png` and `figures/FigC_Alpha_Topomap.png` to confirm the clear alpha peak in occipital sensors.
3. **Artifact Mapping:** Check `figures/FigF_Bad_Channels_Topomap.png` to locate the exact spatial positions of the disconnected frontal electrodes in the Eyes-Open condition.
